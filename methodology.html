Methodology

How learning gets built

Learning doesn’t end at “you can close this window.”

A practical approach to instructional design and multimedia — clarifying the need, partnering with experts, choosing the right format, and checking whether people can actually use what they learned.

1. Intake

Projects start by clarifying the audience, business need, and constraints before building. Who needs to do what differently — and what gets in the way?

2. Stakeholders & SMEs

Close partnership with subject-matter experts and stakeholders for accuracy and buy-in. The goal is usable truth, not a slide dump of everything someone knows.

3. Recommend

Advise on format and approach — course vs video vs resource — based on the learning need, time available, and how people will apply it on the job.

4. ADDIE in the work

Analysis, design, development, implementation, and evaluation show up as a loop, not a waterfall checkbox. Scope and fidelity flex with the problem.

5. Knowledge checks throughout

Practice and feedback inside the experience, not only at the end — flip cards, sorting, scenarios, and other chances to try before a final quiz. (See the Allstate course on Work.)

6. Assessment

Measure what learners can do after the experience — with feedback that reinforces the rule, not just a score.

7. Analytics & follow-through

After launch, review data and feedback to see if training is effective — then iterate. Closing the window isn’t the finish line.
