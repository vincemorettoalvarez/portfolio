Methodology

How learning gets built

Learning doesn’t end when someone can close the window.

A practical path from clarifying the need to checking whether people can use what they learned: intake, experts, the right format, ADDIE, practice, assessment, and follow-through.

01

Intake

Clarify the need before building.

Projects start by clarifying the audience, business need, and constraints. Who needs to do what differently, and what gets in the way?

02

Stakeholders & SMEs

Partner for accuracy and buy-in.

Close partnership with subject matter experts and stakeholders. The goal is usable truth, not a slide dump of everything someone knows.

03

Recommend

Choose the format that fits the job.

Advise on approach (course, video, or resource) based on the learning need, time available, and how people will apply it on the job.

04

ADDIE in the work

Treat the model as a loop, not a checkbox.

Analysis, design, development, implementation, and evaluation show up as a cycle. Scope and fidelity flex with the problem.

05

Knowledge checks throughout

Practice inside the experience, not only at the end.

Flip cards, sorting, scenarios, and other chances to try before a final quiz. See the Allstate course on Work.

06

Assessment

Measure what learners can do afterward.

Feedback that reinforces the rule, not just a score. Closing with clarity matters more than finishing with a percentage.

07

Analytics & follow-through

Review after launch, then iterate.

Look at data and feedback to see if training is effective. Closing the window isn’t the finish line; follow-through is.

The same craft shows up in selected projects: practice inside courses, weekly video learning, and expert interviews shaped for an audience.
