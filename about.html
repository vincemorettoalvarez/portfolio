Where I am now

Sales enablement, built as learning

I’m B2B Sales Enablement Lead at Wayfair Professional (2020–present). I work with Communications and Sales Enablement to turn messy operations into courses, videos, job aids, and the weekly updates remote and hybrid sellers actually read.

I’m also earning an MBA at Weber State University. It gives the learning work more business context, which is the point.

How I show up

  • I design curricula with subject-matter experts so the learning matches the job, not a pile of slides.
  • I write, shoot, and cut training video, including a weekly series, with captions and the brand intact.
  • I run onboarding, small pilots, and follow-up so we can tell if the training stuck.
  • I can hold a room or a Zoom. I spent two years training from the floor before I ever opened Storyline.

From customer education to the field

Helping people learn new devices, then enabling the people who sell them.

I started with customers, including people new to the tech, then moved into field enablement at partner stores. As Field Sales Manager I helped train about 150 reps. That’s where facilitation took root.

Wayfair Professional

One audience. Three jobs.

Outreach, then the training room, then the course file.

I enrolled customers, then built and ran a four-week new-hire program from the front of the room (2018–2020). After that I moved into courses, videos, and comms for the same sales audience.
